# Research lenses

The package separates evidence lenses so answers do not blend consensus, mechanism,
contrarian hypotheses, and risk critique.

| Lens | Role |
|---|---|
| Mainstream | What a conventional clinician/consensus source would likely say. |
| Frontier | Newer mechanistic, omics, quantified-self, or early clinical literature. |
| Edge | High-responsibility, experimental, n-of-1, low-collateral hypotheses. |
| Contrarian | Serious critiques of accepted narratives and overlooked alternatives. |
| Capture | Incentives, funding, endpoint games, guideline conflicts, market shaping. |
| Inversion | What if the default intervention is the risk and non-action is beneficial? |
| Risk | Harms, uncertainty, reversibility, interactions, stop criteria. |

No lens gets automatic authority. The output should make claims atomic and score them by
endpoint quality, absolute effect, replication, mechanism, conflicts, and fit to the profile.
